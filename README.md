# Needle Works

Needle Works is a macOS viewer for embroidery designs in Brother's PES
format. It shows a design's stitches in their thread colors, prints them
with a legend of the threads, and adds Quick Look previews and thumbnails
for PES files in the Finder.

## Features

- Three stitch styles: Plain lines, Shaded thread drawn at its real
  width, and Realistic thread with twist and natural variation.
- Designs are shown on a woven fabric: white, natural, khaki, heather
  gray, chambray, denim and other colors.
- Jump stitches and trims can be shown or hidden.
- The fabric and stitch style chosen for a design are remembered.
  Design files are never changed.
- Printing at actual size or scaled to fit the page, with a legend of
  the threads in sewing order.
- The Design Information panel shows the design's size, stitch count and
  color changes.
- Quick Look previews and Finder thumbnails for PES files.

## Installing

Download the latest zip from
[Releases](https://github.com/ryanlovett/needleworks/releases), unzip it,
and move Needle Works to your Applications folder. It needs macOS 12 or
later. The app isn't notarized, so the first time you open it, go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

## Building

Open `Needle Works.xcodeproj` in Xcode and build the Needle Works scheme.
