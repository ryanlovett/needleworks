# Sample designs

`Needle Works.pes`, `Peace.pes` and `Flower.pes` are sample designs made
for Needle Works, and are covered by its license. They're used for the
screenshots on the project page.

To make them again:

    Designs/make-designs.sh

This builds `DesignGenerator.swift` with the icon's satin stitches from
`Icon/Satin.swift`, and writes the three files here. They come out the
same every time.

The letters are in Cinzel Decorative Bold, the icon's font, satin-stitched
one at a time. The peace sign is white satin over a fill of rows 0.4 mm
apart, and the flower is satin petals, stem and leaves around a filled
center.

The files hold just the PEC section of a PES file: the stitches, the
threads, and the small pictures embroidery machines show. That is all
Needle Works reads. They haven't been tried on a machine or in other
embroidery software.
